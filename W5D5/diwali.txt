Diwali is one of the most popular festivals celebrated in India.
It is known as the Festival of Lights and symbolizes the victory
of good over evil and light over darkness.

During Diwali, people clean and decorate their homes with colorful
lights, candles, and traditional oil lamps called diyas.

Families prepare delicious sweets and special food.
People wear new clothes and exchange gifts with friends and family.

Diwali is a time for happiness, kindness, togetherness, and sharing.
